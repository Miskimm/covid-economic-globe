Live Data Sources & Method
Sources
  • world-atlas v2 — country boundaries (CDN, static)
  • disease.sh — COVID-19 cumulative cases & deaths (live, no-store)
  • World Bank NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG — annual GDP growth 2019–2023 (live, no-store)
Formulas
GDP Shock = y2020 − y2019 (pp)
Recovery = y2023 − y2020 (pp)
Limitations
  • Missing GDP values use local fallback dataset (8 countries).
  • Case counts reflect country-reported figures; reporting standards vary.
