# Week 9 Research Execution (Method-Justified)

## Objective
Execute a mixed-method pass aligned to RQ1-RQ3 in `gap_matrix_rumsfeld.md`, and collect evidence that supports problem definition before committing to a new prototype direction.

## Methods Used and Justification

| Method | Why this method | Gap addressed | Data produced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comparative questionnaire (4-prototype structure) | Produces consistent cross-format measurements | Gap C (preference vs understanding) | Ratings on usability, clarity, linkage understanding, trust, and fatigue |
| Short interpretation prompts (inside survey + follow-up notes) | Tests whether users can explain meaning, not only click UI | Gap A (interpretation validity) | Explanation quality indicators and misunderstanding patterns |
| Desk research / literature scan (argument support layer) | Strengthens claim-evidence chain and avoids solution-first bias | Gap B + tutor critique on justification | Evidence for user pain, interpretation challenges, trust/uncertainty communication |

## Preliminary Findings (From Current Survey Sample)

1. **Small sample warning**: n=4 is directionally useful but not conclusive.
2. **Format signal exists**: responses show clear differences across prototypes in perceived clarity/usability.
3. **Preference vs understanding risk remains**:
   - users can strongly prefer one format,
   - but this does not by itself prove better interpretation quality.
4. **Data priorities are identifiable**:
   - economic change/recovery and health-economy linkage appear repeatedly in selected focus categories.
5. **Action implication**:
   - next cycle must add direct interpretation tasks to complement preference scores.

## Research Quality Controls
- Separate "liked" metrics from "understood" metrics in analysis.
- Keep user group explicit in reporting (no broad stakeholder generalization).
- Tag every major claim with evidence origin (survey/interview/literature).
- Record uncertainty and sample-size limitations in conclusions.
