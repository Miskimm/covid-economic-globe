# Solution 7 (Pre-Prototype): Problem-Framing Interview Script + Templates

> Use this version **before** any interactive prototype exists.  
> Goal: validate problem framing, user needs, and information comprehension paths.

---

## A. Session Setup (No Prototype)

### Materials to prepare

- Concept cards (3-5): each card shows one idea (e.g., country comparison, timeline change, trust/source block).
- One static wireframe sheet (paper/Figma screenshot is fine).
- Metric definition card:
    - "GDP vs 2019" (plain-language definition draft)
    - "Recovery vs 2020" (plain-language definition draft)
- Source transparency card (what data source/method note might look like).

### Intro script (read aloud)

> "Thanks for joining. We are not testing a finished product today.  
> We are testing whether our problem framing and information design direction make sense.  
> There are no right or wrong answers. Please think aloud and tell us what feels clear or confusing."

---

## B. Interview Script (Pre-Prototype)

## Block 1 — User context (3-5 min)

1. "In your coursework/work, when do you need to interpret economic or health data?"
2. "What is currently hard when comparing countries or time periods?"
3. "What mistakes do people often make when reading this kind of data?"

## Block 2 — Concept understanding (5-7 min)

Show concept cards only (no interaction).

4. "Take a few minutes to review these concept cards. Please think aloud: what do you think this information is trying to communicate?"
5. "Which part is clear? Which part is confusing?"
6. "If you had to explain this to a teammate, what would you say in one sentence?"

## Block 3 — Metric interpretation (core, 8-10 min)

Show metric definition card.

7. "In your own words, what does 'GDP vs 2019' mean?"
8. "In your own words, what does 'Recovery vs 2020' mean?"
9. "What is the difference between these two indicators?"
10. "If we only showed one of them, which one would you keep and why?"

## Block 4 — Comparison reasoning (core, 6-8 min)

Show static comparison sheet (two countries, two periods).

11. "Based on this sheet, how would you compare Country A and Country B?"
12. "What evidence would you cite for your conclusion?"
13. "What cannot be concluded from this sheet?"

## Block 5 — Trust and transparency (5 min)

Show source transparency card.

14. "Before trusting these insights, what source/method information do you need?"
15. "What would make you distrust this information?"
16. "Rate trust from 1-5 and explain your reason."

## Block 6 — Priority and next design direction (3-5 min)

17. "Which information block is most necessary for you?"
18. "What should be removed to reduce confusion?"
19. "If we build a first low-fidelity prototype next week, what must it include?"

# 方案 7（原型前）：问题框架访谈脚本 + 模板

> 请在任何交互式原型出现之前使用此版本。

> 目标：验证问题框架、用户需求和信息理解路径。

---

## A. 会话设置（无原型）

### 准备材料

- 概念卡（3-5 张）：每张卡片展示一个想法（例如，国家/地区比较、时间线变化、信任/来源限制）。

- 一张静态线框图（纸质/Figma 截图均可）。

- 指标定义卡：

- “GDP 与 2019 年对比”（简明定义草稿）

- “经济复苏与 2020 年对比”（简明定义草稿）

- 来源透明度卡（数据来源/方法说明示例）。

### 开场白（朗读）

> “感谢您的参与。今天我们不测试成品。

> 我们正在测试我们的问题框架和信息设计方向是否合理。

> 没有对错之分。请大声思考，并告诉我们哪些内容清晰易懂，哪些内容令人困惑。”

---

## B. 访谈脚本（原型前）

## 第一部分 — 用户情境（3-5 分钟）

1. “在您的课程/工作中，您什么时候需要解读经济或健康数据？”

2. “在比较不同国家或时间段时，目前有哪些困难？”

3. “人们在阅读这类数据时经常犯哪些错误？”

## 第二部分 — 概念理解（5-7 分钟）

仅展示概念卡片（不进行互动）。

4. “请花几分钟时间浏览这些概念卡片。请大声思考：您认为这些信息试图传达什么信息？”

5. “哪一部分清楚？哪一部分令人困惑？”

6. “如果你要向队友解释这个，你会用一句话怎么说？”

## 第三部分 — 指标解读（核心，8-10 分钟）

展示指标定义卡。

7. “用你自己的话解释一下‘GDP vs 2019’是什么意思？”

8. “用你自己的话解释一下‘Recovery vs 2020’是什么意思？”

9. “这两个指标有什么区别？”

10. “如果我们只能展示其中一个，你会保留哪个？为什么？”

## 第四部分 — 比较推理（核心，6-8 分钟）

展示静态比较表（两个国家，两个时期）。

11. “根据这张表，你会如何比较A国和B国？”

12. “你会引用哪些证据来支持你的结论？”

13. “从这张表格中无法得出什么结论？”

## 第 5 部分 — 信任与透明度（5 分钟）

展示来源透明度卡。

14. “在信任这些见解之前，您需要哪些来源/方法信息？”

15. “什么会让您不信任这些信息？”

16. “请从 1 到 5 分评价信任度，并解释您的理由。”

## 第 6 部分 — 优先级和下一步设计方向（3-5 分钟）

17. “对您来说，哪个信息块最必要？”

18. “为了减少混淆，应该删除哪些内容？”

19. “如果我们下周构建第一个低保真原型，它必须包含哪些内容？”
