pp
Percentage points — absolute unit of change (5% → 3% = drop of 2 pp).
Baseline
Reference level for comparison. Here: 2019 GDP level.
Trough
Lowest point in an economic cycle. Here: the deepest GDP contraction in 2020.
Correlation
Two variables moving together — does not prove one caused the other.
Causation
One variable directly produces a change in another, proven through controlled evidence.
GDP gap
How far current economic output is below the 2019 pre-pandemic level, in pp.
