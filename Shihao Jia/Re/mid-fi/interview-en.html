Background

Screen 1 — Metric Primer

Read these indicator definitions before exploring the comparison.

ECONOMIC GDP gap from 2019 baseline
How far a country's total economic output (GDP) is from its pre-pandemic level in 2019. Measured in percentage points (pp) — a unit of absolute change, not a proportion.
📊 Example: UK 2020 = −12.0 pp → the UK economy was 12 percentage points below its 2019 size at the worst point of the pandemic.
⚠ Does NOT mean: total GDP in dollars, a percentage drop in GDP growth rate, or anything about health outcomes.
ECONOMIC Economic recovery from 2020 trough
How much the economy has grown back since its lowest point (trough) in 2020. A higher number means more economic ground has been regained — not a health or wellbeing measure.
📊 Example: UK 2023 = +8.9 pp → the UK economy has grown back 8.9 percentage points from its 2020 low point.
⚠ Does NOT mean: recovery from illness, improvement in COVID case numbers, or that the economy is above its 2019 level.

Quick Glossary

pp
Percentage points — a unit of absolute change between two values (e.g., 5% → 3% is a drop of 2 pp).
Baseline
The reference level everything is compared against. Here: 2019 GDP = the baseline.
Trough
The lowest point in an economic cycle. Here: the sharpest GDP contraction in 2020.
