# Week 9 Solution (Three-part format)

## 1) Summary
Based on the multi-person discussion, the team identified a core process issue: we previously built prototypes first and then tried to retrofit the problem and rationale. After A01 feedback, we now recognize that this approach weakened the argument quality and reduced research validity. The immediate direction is to reset from problem definition and research questions, then derive solution options from evidence.

## 2) Action Items
- Consolidate and print evidence artifacts for traceability:
  - interview records;
  - questionnaire results;
  - supporting literature/paper references;
  - a clear process diagram showing incorrect vs corrected workflow.
- Write Week 9 reflection in sequence:
  1) what happened in last testing;
  2) which process choices hindered data quality;
  3) what major problem A01 revealed;
  4) how the new research question changes our method.
- Reopen problem framing and run focused research instead of adding features.
- Keep solution space open (do not constrain to existing globe/global format).
- Align team execution to immediate deadlines (today/tomorrow deliverables, member-specific due dates).

## 3) Next Week Plan
- Finalize a revised research question and problem statement this week.
- Produce at least one newly justified solution direction grounded in evidence.
- Run next-round testing on the new direction (not only on the legacy prototype).
- Compare findings against prior assumptions (for example: "immersive is preferred" vs "actually improves understanding").
- Use results to decide whether to pivot, combine, or retire current prototype paths.

# Week 9 解决方案（三段格式）

## 1) 总结
基于多人讨论，团队识别出一个核心流程问题：我们之前是先做原型，再去补问题与论证。A01 反馈后，我们确认这种做法会削弱论证质量并降低研究有效性。当前的直接方向是从问题定义与研究问题重新开始，再由证据推导方案选项。

## 2) 行动项
- 整理并打印可追溯的证据材料：
  - 访谈记录；
  - 问卷结果；
  - 支撑性文献/论文引用；
  - 清晰展示“错误流程 vs 修正流程”的过程图。
- 按顺序完成 Week 9 反思：
  1) 上次测试发生了什么；
  2) 哪些流程选择影响了数据质量；
  3) A01 暴露出的关键问题是什么；
  4) 新研究问题如何改变方法。
- 重新打开问题框架并开展聚焦研究，而不是继续加功能。
- 保持方案空间开放（不被现有 globe/global 形式限制）。
- 按近期节点推进团队执行（今天/明天交付，成员各自截止时间）。

## 3) 下周计划
- 本周内定稿修订后的研究问题与问题陈述。
- 基于证据产出至少一个新近被论证过的方案方向。
- 围绕新方向开展下一轮测试（不只测试旧原型）。
- 将结果与既有假设对照（例如“沉浸式更受欢迎”是否等于“理解更好”）。
- 根据证据决定是转向、融合，还是淘汰现有原型路径。
