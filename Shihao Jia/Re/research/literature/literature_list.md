# Literature List — DECO7180 COVID-19 × Global Economy

Compiled for: user definition, problem framing, and design necessity argument.  
Each entry includes: title, source, year, and one-line relevance note.

---

## Category 1: COVID-19 and Macroeconomic Impact (Background Grounding)

1. **The Economic Impacts of COVID-19: Evidence from a New Public Database Built Using Private Sector Data**  
   Chetty, R. et al. | NBER / Quarterly Journal of Economics | 2024  
   → Establishes cross-country economic shock/recovery patterns as valid and complex; supports the project's core data scope.

2. **The Impact of COVID-19 Pandemic on the World's Major Economies: Based on a Multi-country and Multi-sector CGE Model**  
   Frontiers in Public Health | 2024  
   → Shows differentiated economic impact across regions; supports cross-country comparison as a meaningful user task.

3. **Understanding Structural Effects of COVID-19 on the Global Economy**  
   OECD | 2022  
   → Provides structural framing (labor productivity, government policy) for why economic recovery metrics vary; supports metric justification.

## Category 2: Data Comprehension and Misinterpretation (User Pain Evidence)

4. **Interpreting Time-Series COVID Data: Reasoning Biases, Risk Perception, and Support for Public Health Measures**  
   Scientific Reports (Nature) | 2021  
   → Documents that even educated users misread dynamic COVID time-series data; directly supports the user pain point of misinterpreting temporal health indicators.

5. **Preferred Information Source Correlates to COVID-19 Risk Misperception**  
   PMC / PubMed | 2023  
   → Shows that information-source habits correlate with systematic misperception; supports the argument that users need better interpretation support.

6. **Understanding of Numerical Information during the COVID-19 Pandemic**  
   Europe PMC | 2021  
   → Demonstrates widespread numerical misunderstanding across user groups; supports that non-expert users require scaffolded interpretation, not raw data dumps.

7. **How Do People Process Different Representations of Statistical Information? Insights into Cognitive Effort, Representational Inconsistencies, and Individual Differences**  
   PMC | 2023  
   → Shows that format choice (graphic vs numeric) materially affects cognitive load and decision quality; supports design justification for clear metric presentation.

## Category 3: Trust and Credibility in Data Visualization (Trust Gap Evidence)

8. **Vistrust: a Multidimensional Framework and Empirical Study of Trust in Data Visualizations**  
   IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics / arXiv | 2023  
   → Establishes that trust in visualization is multidimensional (cognitive + affective, design + data); justifies why source transparency and design credibility cues matter.

9. **Visualizing Trust: How Chart Embellishments Influence Perceptions of Credibility**  
   PubMed / IEEE VIS | 2025  
   → Shows specific design choices (color, bar style, font) shift perceived credibility; supports R3 (trust transparency) in Design Brief v2.

10. **Trust Dimensions in Data Visualization: Credibility, Clarity, Reliability, Familiarity, and Confidence**  
    arXiv (vis-related) | 2023  
    → Provides five-dimension trust framework applicable to prototype evaluation; supports measurement design for trust-related testing.

---

## Category 4: Non-Expert Users and Economic Data (User Definition Support)

11. **Cognitive Load in Data-driven Non-expert Decision Making**  
    CBS Research Portal | 2022  
    → Shows lower domain knowledge increases cognitive load and reduces decision quality for non-experts; directly supports primary user definition (students interpreting economic data).

12. **The Framework of Improving the Analytical Interaction in the Visualization of Economic Data for General Public**  
    FHP | 2022  
    → Argues current tools lack analytical interaction for novice users, creating an interpretation gap; directly supports the design opportunity argument.

13. **Use of Visualization Tools to Improve Financial Knowledge: An Experimental Approach**  
    ERIC | 2021  
    → Demonstrates visualization + text outperforms text-only in improving economic comprehension; supports the necessity argument for an interpretation-centered visual tool.

---

## Category 5: Student Users and Data Literacy in Higher Education (Primary User Context)

14. **Developing Students' Analytical and Data Presentation Skills Using Infographic Assignments in Principles of Macroeconomics**  
    Journal of Economics Teaching | 2022  
    → Shows students in economics-adjacent courses develop better analytical skills through visual tasks; validates primary user group (students needing to justify economic arguments).

15. **Telling My Story: Applying Storytelling to Complex Economic Data**  
    Journal of Economics Teaching | 2023  
    → Demonstrates that narrative + data structures improve student confidence and motivation in macroeconomics; supports Option B (narrative evidence flow prototype).

16. **Improving Student Comprehension through Interactive Model Visualization**  
    International Review of Economics Education | 2024  
    → Interactive visualization helps students approach economic problems more like experts; supports design requirement R1 (interpretation clarity).
