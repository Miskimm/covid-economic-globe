## Tool 1: Interview Script (RQ1 + RQ3)

### Pre-session instructions (read to participant)
> "Thank you for joining. We are testing a prototype that shows COVID-19 and economic data. We are not testing your knowledge — there are no right or wrong answers. We want to understand how you think through the information. Please speak your thoughts out loud as you go. The session will take about 20–30 minutes."

---

### Block 1 — Warm-up (3 min)
*Goal: establish baseline familiarity, do not explain any interface yet.*

1. "What course are you currently studying? Do you regularly encounter economic or health data in your coursework?"
2. "Have you looked at any COVID data dashboards or visualisations before? What was that experience like?"

---

### Block 2 — Free exploration (5 min)
*Give participant access to the prototype. Do not explain anything.*

3. "Take a few minutes to explore. Tell me out loud what you see and what you think it shows."

Facilitator notes: observe what they look at first (globe / timeline / side panel), what they ignore.

---

### Block 3 — Interpretation tasks (10 min, core data for RQ1)
*Ask one at a time. Do not correct them. Record exact words.*

4. "Can you point to where it shows the economic information? What does that number mean to you in plain language?"

5. "This indicator is called 'GDP vs 2019'. In your own words, what do you think that means? What is it comparing?"
   - If they answer confidently: "How sure are you? Can you show me on the screen what led you to that conclusion?"
   - If they seem unsure: "Just your best guess is fine — what does your gut tell you?"

6. "Now look at 'Recovery vs 2020'. What does that mean? How is it different from the first one?"

7. "Pick two countries. Tell me which one had a worse economic outcome during the pandemic and why. Where on the screen does that information come from?"
   - Follow-up: "If someone asked you to write this up as an argument in an assignment, what evidence from this screen would you cite?"

---

### Block 4 — Causation boundary task (5 min, core data for RQ3)
*Do not lead. Record exact quote.*

8. "Looking at this screen, if a country had a high number of cases AND a large GDP drop in the same year — what conclusion would you draw about the relationship between those two things?"
   - Follow-up: "Is that something the data can actually prove, or is it something you are inferring?"

9. "Is there anything this data cannot tell you? What would you need to know that is not shown here?"

---

### Block 5 — Trust and source (5 min, core data for RQ3)
*Show source/method note if available on interface.*

10. "Do you notice any information about where this data comes from? Does that matter to you?"
11. "On a scale of 1 to 5, how much do you trust the numbers shown here? What is your main reason?"
12. "If you were going to use this in an assignment, would you be comfortable citing it? Why or why not?"

---

### Block 6 — Preference (collect LAST, after all tasks)
*Only after all interpretation tasks are complete.*

13. "Now that you have used this prototype, overall how would you rate it on a scale of 1 to 5 for how useful it felt?"
14. "What was the most helpful part? What would you change?"

**Error type definitions:**
- **Metric confusion** — mixed up what the indicator measures (e.g., thought GDP vs 2019 = total GDP, not growth rate gap)
- **Time axis confusion** — misread which time period is being compared
- **False causation** — concluded health data caused economic data without acknowledging correlation boundary
- **Selective attention** — only read health indicators, ignored economic panel (or vice versa)
- **Overconfidence** — stated conclusion with high certainty but was factually wrong

### 工具 1：访谈脚本（RQ1 + RQ3）

**开始前念给参与者（开场白）**

> 感谢你参与。我们在测试一个展示新冠疫情与经济数据的原型。**不是在考你的知识**——没有对错答案。我们想了解你如何理解这些信息。请一边操作一边尽量把想法说出来。整场大约 20–30 分钟。

**模块一 —— 热身（约 3 分钟）**  
目标：了解背景，此时还不要讲解界面。

1. 「你目前在读什么课程？课业里会经常碰到经济数据或健康数据吗？」  
2. 「你以前看过新冠相关的数据面板或可视化吗？体验怎么样？」

**模块二 —— 自由探索（约 5 分钟）**  
把原型交给参与者，**不要提前讲解**。

3. 「请先随意探索几分钟，一边操作一边说出你看到什么、你认为它在表达什么。」  

主持笔记：先看哪里（地球仪 / 时间轴 / 侧栏）、忽略了什么。

**模块三 —— 解读任务（约 10 分钟，RQ1 核心）**  
逐题问，**不要当场纠正**；尽量记下原话。

4. 「你能指一下经济信息在界面哪里吗？对你来说，那个数字用大白话是什么意思？」  
5. 「这个指标叫 GDP vs 2019。用你自己的话说，你觉得它在比较什么？」  
   - 若对方答得很自信：「你有多确定？能在屏幕上指给我看，你凭什么得出这个结论吗？」  
   - 若显得不确定：「没关系，猜也行——你的直觉是什么？」  
6. 「再看 Recovery vs 2020，你觉得是什么意思？和上一个指标有什么不一样？」  
7. 「任选两个国家，说说疫情期间哪一个经济状况更糟，为什么？你是从屏幕哪里看到这个信息的？」  
   - 追问：「如果有人要你写作业论证这个观点，你会引用屏幕上哪些证据？」

**模块四 —— 因果边界任务（约 5 分钟，RQ3 核心）**  
不要诱导；记下原话。

8. 「看这页界面：若某国同年病例很多、GDP 跌幅也很大——你会怎么理解这两者之间的关系？」  
   - 追问：「这是数据本身能证明的，还是你在推断？」  
9. 「有哪些问题是这份数据**没法告诉你的**？你还想知道但这里没有的信息是什么？」

**模块五 —— 信任与来源（约 5 分钟，RQ3 核心）**  
若界面上有来源/方法说明，可示意参与者注意。

10. 「你有没有注意到数据从哪里来的？这对你重要吗？」  
11. 「如果 1 到 5 分评价你对这些数字的信任程度，你会打几分？主要原因是什么？」  
12. 「如果你要在作业里引用这个工具里的数字，你愿意吗？为什么愿意或不愿意？」

**模块六 —— 偏好（务必放在最后）**  
所有解读类任务结束后再问。

13. 「用过这个原型之后，整体有用程度你会打几分（1–5）？」  
14. 「哪一部分最有帮助？你会改什么？」

**会后主持填写（小结字段）**

- 会话编号：______  
- 参与者背景：______  
- 任务 5（GDP vs 2019）——正确 / 部分正确 / 错误：______  
- 任务 6（Recovery vs 2020）——正确 / 部分正确 / 错误：______  
- 任务 7（跨国比较）——是否引用界面证据 / 未引用：______  
- 任务 8（因果边界）——正确识别局限 / 把相关当成因果：______  
- 信任分（任务 11）：______  
- 关键原话（尽量逐字）：______

**错误类型说明**

- **指标混淆**：说不清指标在量什么（例如把「相较 2019 的 GDP」当成「GDP 总量」）。  
- **时间轴混淆**：搞错比较的是哪一段时期。  
- **虚假因果**：在边界未说明的情况下断言健康数据「导致」经济数据变化。  
- **选择性注意**：只看健康或只看经济一侧。  
- **过度自信**：语气很确定但事实错误或未涵盖边界。
