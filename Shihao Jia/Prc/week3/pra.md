Overview

The DECO7180 Tuesday Tutor session focused on preparing students for their first assessment by discussing research methods and secondary research practices. The session emphasized the importance of framing research questions, understanding the broader context, and identifying relevant stakeholders. Students were encouraged to use resources like the ACM Digital Library, UQ Library, and Google Scholar for academic literature. The discussion also covered the potential risks and ethical considerations of their projects, particularly in the context of whistleblowing and corruption in the construction industry. The session concluded with a collaborative exercise to refine team research questions and project directions.
