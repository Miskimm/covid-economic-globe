## 固定话术（中英各一句）

### 项目定位

- **中**：这不是疫情仪表盘，而是帮学生在作业里**有证据地解释**健康–经济联动。  
- **En**: This is an evidence-backed **interpretation** tool, not a dashboard for browsing numbers.

### 地球旋转

- **中**：旋转是为了**全球探索感**和暗示可交互；**读数与论证**在侧栏和对比里；已知旋转负担，所以加搜索。  
- **En**: Rotation signals a **global, explorable** view; **reading and arguing** happen in panels and compare mode—we added search because rotation alone was a burden.

### 相关 ≠ 因果

- **中**：数据能支持「同期英国经济收缩更深」，不能证明「病例导致了 GDP」。  
- **En**: The data supports a **deeper UK contraction in 2020**; it does **not** prove COVID cases **caused** GDP to fall.

### Recovery 歧义

- **中**：Recovery 指自 2020 经济低谷的反弹，**不是**公共卫生意义上的康复。  
- **En**: **Economic recovery** means rebound from the 2020 **GDP trough**, not health recovery.

### 样本局限

- **中**：我们是小样本知情同意访谈，结论是**设计方向性**的，不是全国代表性统计。  
- **En**: We ran **small-n, consented** think-aloud sessions—findings are **directional for design**, not population inference.

### 个人分工

- **中**：我负责**来源可追溯**和**多国对比**，直接对应测试里的信任校准和证据引用问题。  
- **En**: I own **provenance** and **multi-region comparison**, mapped to trust calibration and citation failures in testing.

## 对比演示「金句」（UK vs AU）

> "In 2020 the UK's GDP gap was **−12.0 percentage points** below the 2019 baseline, versus **−3.7** for Australia—more than three times deeper. By 2023 Australia was nearly back at **−0.3 pp**, while the UK was still **−1.5 pp**. Higher UK **recovery** from the trough does not mean the UK caught up to 2019—it fell from a much deeper hole."

> "I trust it **4 out of 5** because World Bank and OWID are named with formula transparency, but I would not give 5 until I've noted **cross-country case reporting differences** and **GDP revision lag**—those limitations affect how far I can push the comparison."

| 少说 | 改说 |
| --- | --- |
| "Users loved the globe" | "Globe increased engagement; scaffolding increased **accurate interpretation**" |
| "We proved causation" | "We show **patterns**; causation needs other evidence" |
| "Statistically significant" (n=6) | "**Directional** finding / consistent pattern" |
| "AI knows the answer" | "AI explains **on-screen** metrics under constraints" |

1. **User** — non-expert students, coursework

3. **Test** — one failure (metric / causation / trust)  
4. **Fix** — one mechanism (badge / compare / source gate)
