Data Sources & Method
Evidence traceability

Retrieved: —

Data Sources
  • world-atlas v2 — country boundaries (TopoJSON, CDN)
  • disease.sh — COVID-19 cases & deaths by country (API docs, live when available)
  • World Bank NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG — annual GDP growth %, 2019–2023 (indicator page)
How We Combine Data
  • Countries align on ISO3 across the map, disease.sh, and World Bank.
  • Health trends use daily cases on the timeline.
  • GDP growth is annual and does not change day-to-day on the slider.
Limitations
  • Displaying local fallback only — live APIs did not load.
  • Patterns on screen do not prove that infections directly caused economic change.
  • Cross-country case totals are not directly comparable; reporting standards vary.
