COVID-19 and GDP Growth in One View

This prototype aggregates WHO daily COVID-19 records into annual totals and aligns them with the World Bank “GDP growth (annual %)” indicator to explore how pandemic peaks relate to economic contraction and recovery.

Data sources
GDP: World Bank WDI, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG. The indicator page shows coverage from 1961 to 2024 and is licensed under CC BY-4.0. Indicators API V2 uses the /v2 path and requires no API key. WHO COVID-19 records are aggregated from daily rows; this prototype uses annual totals for 2020-2025.
